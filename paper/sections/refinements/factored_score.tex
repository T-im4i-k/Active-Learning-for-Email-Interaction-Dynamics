\subsection{Factored Score with Scheduled Coefficients}
\label{sec:factored-score}

The additive score in Eq.~\eqref{eq:score} ties exploration solely to
historic data ($\phi_j p_j$) and exploitation solely to current data
($f_j(t)$). We instead factor it into a probability-of-opening term and a
utility-of-opening term, each with its own coefficient:
\begin{align}
\pi_j(t) &= \alpha(t)\,\phi_j + \big(1-\alpha(t)\big)\,f_j(t),
  \label{eq:pi}\\
u_j(t) &= \beta(t)\,p_j + \big(1-\beta(t)\big),
  \label{eq:u}\\
s_j(t) &= \pi_j(t)\,u_j(t).
  \label{eq:s-factored}
\end{align}
$\pi_j(t)$ blends historic and current opening-probability estimates;
$u_j(t)$ blends the direct value of an open (the constant $1$) with its
indirect information value $p_j$. The refinements of
Sections~\ref{sec:recency-phi}--\ref{sec:variance-p} enter $\phi_j$,
$f_j(t)$ and $p_j$ unchanged.

\paragraph{Scheduling.}
The indirect value of an open shrinks as the sending budget is spent, and
$f_j(t)$ becomes reliable as opens accumulate. Echoing decaying exploration
in bandits \citep{auer2002finite}, we therefore schedule $\beta$ on
$\mu = N(t)/N$, the fraction of the sending budget used, and $\alpha$ on
$\tilde o = o(t)/m$, the fraction of recipients who have opened, with
$o(t) = x(t)^\top\mathbf{1}$.

\emph{Send-volume-based $\beta$ (log-linear).} Analogous to geometric
cooling in simulated annealing \citep{mahdi2017performance},
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:beta-loglinear}
\beta(\mu) = \beta_0^{\,1-\mu}\,\beta_1^{\,\mu},
\qquad 0<\beta_1\le\beta_0\le 1,
\end{equation}
so $\log\beta$ is linear in $\mu$ and decays at a constant relative rate.

\emph{Open-rate-based $\alpha$ (hyperbolic).}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:alpha-hyperbolic}
\alpha(\tilde o) = \frac{\kappa}{\tilde o + \kappa},
\qquad \kappa>0.
\end{equation}
Here $\alpha(0)=1$, since $f_j(t)$ carries no information before any opens,
and $\alpha(\kappa)=\tfrac12$. As $\tilde o$ is bounded by the overall open
rate ($9.1\%$ in \citealp{zid2025active}), $\kappa$ lives on that scale.
