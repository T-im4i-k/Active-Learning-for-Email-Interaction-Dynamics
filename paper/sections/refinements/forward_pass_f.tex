\subsection{Forward-Pass Definition of $f_j(t)$}
\label{sec:forward-f}

$f_j(t) = \bar x(t)^\top\Sigma_{:j}$ is a linear combination of individual
influences $\Sigma_{i,j}$ and therefore captures only the \emph{average}
effect of openers, never their joint interaction. We instead pass the full
current state through the trained network, in the spirit of nonlinear
autoencoder-based collaborative-filtering predictors such as AutoRec
\citep{sedhain2015autorec}, and control its confidence by scaling the logit
with $\gamma>0$, i.e.\ by applying a softmax-style temperature $1/\gamma$
\citep{guo2017calibration}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:f-forward}
f_j(t) = \sigma\big(\gamma\, z_j(t)\big),
\qquad
z_j(t) = x(t)^\top B_{E,D}\,e_j.
\end{equation}
For $\gamma=1$ this is the plain forward pass $f_{\SAE}(x(t))\,e_j$. Because
$\sigma$ is nonlinear, $\sigma(z_j(t)) \ne x(t)^\top\Sigma_{:j}$ in general
(equality holds only for one-hot $x(t)$), so Eq.~\eqref{eq:f-forward} is a
genuinely different, jointly-conditioned probability estimate rather than a
relabeling of Eq.~\eqref{eq:phi-p-f}.

\paragraph{Logit scaling.}
Since $\sigma(0)=\tfrac12$, the neutral prior of the original SAE is
preserved for every $\gamma$. For $\gamma<1$ scores contract toward
$\tfrac12$, and for $\gamma>1$ they
move away from it. Rescaling is
warranted because the SAE is trained on complete rows of $X$, whereas
$x(t)$ contains only the opens observed so far, so the confidence the
network assigns to $z_j(t)$ cannot be taken at face value. We keep $\gamma$
constant: as opens accumulate, $x(t)$ activates more rows of $B_{E,D}$, with
entries of either sign, so $z_j(t)$ already evolves with the evidence and
$\gamma$ acts only as a constant temperature on top of it.
