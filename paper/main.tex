% Here goes the abstract
\begin{abstract}
This paper extends the SAE--Thompson Sampling framework of
\citet{zid2025active} for cold-start recipient selection in active email
outreach. Several components of the original model --- a fixed
exploration/exploitation coefficient, an unweighted historic open rate, a
linear aggregation for the current-state confidence term, training on
binary labels only, and a shallow autoencoder architecture --- were fixed
by the original authors for simplicity. We revisit each of these choices
and propose concrete, mathematically grounded refinements, and report
experimental results for each proposal against a reproduced baseline.
\end{abstract}

% Research highlights
\begin{highlights}
\item Extends the SAE--Thompson Sampling framework for active email outreach of \citet{zid2025active}.
\item Proposes dynamic $\alpha$-scheduling, a recency-weighted historic open rate, a nonlinear forward-pass confidence term, a variance-based influence score, a factored score function, time-to-open signal, and a deep autoencoder.
\item Reports experimental results for each proposal against a reproduced baseline.
\end{highlights}
